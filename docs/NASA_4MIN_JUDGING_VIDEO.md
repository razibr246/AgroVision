# Judging videos: one farmer, one field, one decision

- **Challenge:** Field Shift: Adapting Farms with NASA Data · **Project:** FieldShift 360 · **Team:** BRM AstraX
- **Live site:** https://fieldshift360.vercel.app · **Code:** https://github.com/razibr246/fieldshift360
- **Local judging video:** 240 seconds, due 13 November 2026, 6:30 PM. **Global video:** 30 seconds, due 14 November,
  12:00 PM. Check both times in the participant portal.

Both videos tell one story: Rahim, a sample farmer in Godagari on the Barind, grows Boro on a field that is drying
out. FieldShift 360 shows him whether to keep it, what ten years of each rotation would do to his soil and water,
and whether the answer holds in the 2040s. Everything else in the app (the games, the other countries, AR and VR)
is left out, or gets one sentence at the end.

## Rules for both videos

- **Burned-in English subtitles** for every word spoken in Bangla.
- **No faces or voices of anyone under 18.** If a child appears in a field shot, cut it.
- **Name each NASA dataset on screen as it appears**, in a lower-third: *NASA POWER*, *GPM IMERG*, *NEX-GDDP-CMIP6*,
  *MODIS*, *GRACE*.
- **Read every number off the live screen** and say it as the screen shows it. Do not add a number that is not on
  the screen. `[read from screen]` below marks where.
- Say that Rahim and his field are **samples**; the weather, soil estimate, prices and yields are real data.
- Credit any third-party photo or music on the closing card.
- Do not say: validated, field-proven, fully offline, Tier 2, a test count other than the one in
  `docs/SUBMISSION.md`, or anything about a usability score.

## Recording

1. Use the live site, signed in as the sample farmer Rahim (Godagari), on a phone-sized window for the farmer screens.
2. Record each row as its own clip (OBS Studio or the phone's screen recorder); narrate afterwards.
3. Before exporting, check every on-screen number against the live site once more; if a number has changed, re-read it.
4. Export 1080p, burn in the English subtitles, and upload as YouTube **Unlisted**. Open the link in a private
   window before pasting it into the project page.
